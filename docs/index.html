GeoNews puts news incidents on a map and groups follow-up articles into event timelines. You can browse without signing in; sign in to contribute.

Browse and search

  • Drag the map to pan and zoom to see more individual markers. Click a cluster to zoom into its incidents; click a marker to open its article card.
  • Use Map style to switch between OpenStreetMap, CARTO Light, CARTO Dark and Esri Satellite. Your choice is remembered in this browser; switching styles keeps incident pins and your map position.
  • Use the Location search tab for an address or place. Use Incidents to search loaded incidents by title, summary, category or author.
  • The category filter and start/end dates control which incident pins appear. Presets give quick date ranges; use the date inputs for a specific range.
  • On desktop, the sidebar lists incidents in the selected range. The bottom timeline offers week/month views with previous, next and current-period controls. Weeks start on Monday at 00:00 Europe/Oslo and end at the following Monday at 00:00. Clicking an event highlights its incident and associated updates.
  • Direct Link copies a link to an incident. Publication dates and times are shown in Europe/Oslo.

Add an incident

  1. Sign in using Login / Register.
  2. Click the incident location on the map to open Add News Article.
  3. Paste the specific article URL. Metadata can fill in the title, summary, image, publication date/time and a suggested category.
  4. Check the details and pin coordinates. If a place is detected, you can choose to move the pin to the suggested location.
  5. Choose a category and marker, verify casualty counts and publication time, then click Save Article.

Use a link to the actual news article rather than a news site's homepage: refresh reads information from the saved URL.

Categories and marker emojis

Category describes the incident and is used by filters. Selecting a category also chooses its default marker—for example 🔥 for Fires or 🗡️ for Violent Crime.

Hazard categories include ☢️ Nuclear & Radiation and ☣️ Biological Hazard.

You can then override the marker in Map Marker Emoji without changing the category. The picker lists Category defaults first, followed by Additional markers beneath a divider: vehicles, police, emergency services, death and weapons.

Selecting another category resets the marker to that category's default. Opening an existing article preserves its saved marker, as do metadata suggestions and bulk category refreshes.

Edit and refresh data

Open an incident card and choose Edit to change its details, or Refresh Data to fetch current metadata from its saved URL. These controls are available for articles you can edit; admins and moderators can edit other users' articles.

Refresh displays stored and fetched values for changed fields. Use Keep Old beside a field to retain the stored value, and click Save Article to save your reviewed changes. Refreshing metadata preserves the saved marker.

Missing metadata is kept rather than guessed. Category and casualty suggestions are inferred from text: review them before saving. A missing publication time does not establish a clock time; check the retained value.

Add a follow-up article to a timeline

  1. Open an incident card and click + Add Update.
  2. Paste the follow-up article URL and check its headline, source name, publication date and time.
  3. Click Add Update. It appears in that incident's event timeline.

Follow-up articles have their own URL, headline and publication time. They share the incident's pin; categories and marker emojis belong to the master incident.

Move a pin

If you can edit an incident, drag its pin to a new location. A confirmation shows the old and new coordinates. Confirm to save, or cancel to restore the original position. A failed save also restores the original position.

Admin bulk refresh

Signed-in admins have a Bulk update link beside their account controls. This tool lists all incidents, regardless of date, with timeline articles grouped beneath each incident.

  1. Select incidents and individual articles, or select all. Choose master articles, timeline articles, or both.
  2. Select the types of information to refresh. Timeline articles support title, publication date and time.
  3. Click Scan selected articles. Each article is fetched from its own saved URL; scanning does not save changes.
  4. Review stored/fetched values below each article. Deselect any changes you want to skip.
  5. Click Apply selected changes. Per-article results show successful saves and failures.

The Stop button finishes the current article, then stops. Missing information keeps stored values. Date and time can be updated independently; pin coordinates and chosen markers are preserved. If a selected stored field or URL changed since preview, scan again to resolve the conflict.
